Travel without guessing.

Halo Journeys plans your whole trip around your needs, then shows what is actually accessible: facts from the web with their sources, and what real travellers have verified on Monad. No invented claims.

Two ideas that make it different

Accessibility information you can trust.

🔎

1 · Honest research

Tell it your trip and your needs, by typing or speaking. The agent researches every airport, station, hotel and sight, and shows each fact with its source link. If nothing reliable is found, it says “unknown” instead of guessing.

2 · Proof from real travellers, on-chain

Travellers who have been there attest what they really found, and can mark “the venue confirmed this in writing”. It lives on Monad, so nobody can quietly edit it. Each place gets an honest badge.

How it works

Tell it. Check it. Confirm it.

1

Tell it

“3 days Pune to Goa, I use a wheelchair.” Type it or say it, in English or Hindi.

2

It checks

In about 20 seconds: a day-by-day plan, every place researched with sources, cross-checked against Monad.

3

You confirm

Send the ready-made message to the venue. Been there? Attest what you found, and help the next traveller.

Run it yourself

Plan any trip live on your Mac.

git clone https://github.com/callmeharshh/halo
cd halo && ./setup.sh
# add your OpenAI key to .env
source .venv/bin/activate
python travel/server.py

Halo Journeys was built at Monad Blitz Pune V3. The idea of an AI agent for disabled travellers was inspired by Inclusive Travel Agent by Coders Beyond Borders; this is our own implementation, and our addition is the traveller-verified on-chain registry and source-linked research. We started from GemGuide by Sourajit (voice, gaze and screen vision).

Accessibility information comes from public web sources and traveller attestations. It can be wrong or out of date: always confirm with the venue. Testnet demo.
