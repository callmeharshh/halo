Two ideas that make it different

Help that can’t be tricked.

🛡️

1 · Scam Shield

Just ask “Is this message safe?” Halo reads what is on the screen and tells you in plain words, in English or Hindi: fake KYC, OTP requests, “pay now” pressure.

URGENT! Your bank account will be BLOCKED. Share your OTP now.
⚠️ This looks like a scam. Don’t share your OTP. Ask your family first.
Illustration
🔒

2 · A wallet no scammer can drain

A family member sets the rules on-chain: how much per payment, per day, and to whom. Halo and the person can only spend inside those rules. If anyone is tricked, the contract simply says no.

Run it yourself

Five commands on a Mac.

git clone https://github.com/callmeharshh/halo
cd halo && ./setup.sh
# add your OpenAI key and a throwaway testnet wallet key to .env
source .venv/bin/activate
python halo.py

Halo was built at Monad Blitz Pune V3. It builds on the open-source GemGuide by Sourajit, whose voice, gaze and screen-vision foundation we ported to macOS. We added the Scam Shield, the Guardian, and the family-owned GuardVault contract on Monad.

Testnet demo. Not financial advice and not a production wallet.
